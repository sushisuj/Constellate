# Vendor Service Agreement (Sample — Fictional, for Testing Only)

This document is entirely fictional, generated to test Constellate's
upload and question-answering pipeline. Any resemblance to a real
contract is coincidental.

This Vendor Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between
Nimbus Cloud Storage, Inc. ("Vendor") and Aldergate Retail Group LLC
("Client").

## Term and Termination

### 2.1 Initial Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twenty-four
(24) months, renewing automatically for successive twelve (12) month
periods unless either party gives ninety (90) days' written notice of
non-renewal.

### 2.2 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with one
hundred twenty (120) days' written notice. Client remains responsible for
all fees accrued through the termination date.

### 9.1 Termination for Breach

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice
if the other party materially breaches any term and fails to cure that
breach within thirty (30) days of receiving notice of it. A breach of the
data retrieval and return obligations in Section 4.2 is deemed material
for purposes of this clause.

## Data Handling

### 4.1 Data Processing

Vendor will process Client Data solely to provide the services described
in this Agreement and will not use Client Data for any other purpose,
including training third-party models, without Client's prior written
consent.

### 4.2 Data Retrieval and Return

Upon termination of this Agreement for any reason, Vendor shall provide
Client with a complete export of all Client Data in a commonly used,
machine-readable format within thirty (30) days of the termination date.
Vendor will permanently delete all copies of Client Data from its systems
within sixty (60) days of the termination date, except where retention is
required by law.

## Service Level Agreement

### 9.1 Uptime Commitment

Vendor commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for the hosted service, excluding
scheduled maintenance windows announced at least forty-eight (48) hours
in advance.

### 9.2 Service Credits

If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, Client is entitled to a service
credit equal to 5% of that month's fees for each full percentage point
below the commitment, up to a maximum credit of 30% of that month's fees.

### 9.3 Early-Termination Penalty

If Client terminates this Agreement for Vendor's uncured breach under
Section 9.1 (Termination for Breach), any early-termination fee that
would otherwise apply under Section 2.2 is waived in full.

## Confidentiality

### 6.1 Confidential Information

Each party agrees to protect the other's confidential information using
at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential
information, and no less than reasonable care, for three (3) years after
disclosure.

## Limitation of Liability

### 7.1 Liability Cap

Except for breaches of confidentiality or indemnification obligations,
neither party's total liability under this Agreement will exceed the
total fees paid by Client in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## Payment Terms

### 8.1 Fees and Invoicing

Client will pay Vendor's invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.
Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate
permitted by law, whichever is lower.

## Governing Law

### 10.1 Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without
regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.
